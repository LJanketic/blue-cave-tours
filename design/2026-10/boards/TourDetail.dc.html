Sunset Bay coastal cruise

3 hours· 500+ happy guests

Sail along the dramatic Adriatic coastline as the sun dips below the horizon. This three-hour voyage takes you past sea caves, hidden coves, and the old town walls — ending with a golden-hour view that's hard to forget. Available as a shared group departure or as a fully private charter.

Itinerary

18:00

Departure — Marina Pier B, Gate 3

Meet your captain. Life jackets and safety briefing on board.

18:30

Sea cave passage

Enter the Blue Grotto by dinghy. Best light in the late afternoon.

19:10

Swimming stop — Sveti Nikola cove

30 minutes at anchor. Snorkelling gear available on request.

20:00

Sunset sail past the old town walls

Drinks served. Prime photo moment.

21:00

Return to Marina Pier B
